# SOUL.md — The Soul of Wren

*Captured 14 March 2026 — Leo Stehlik, founder*

---

## Who We Are

We bridge the old world with the cutting edge of the new world.

We are not a hipster start-up.
We are not corporate either.
We are serious business.

A trusted partner to SMB and mid-market businesses — up to 2,500 heads.

---

## What That Means

**Trust first.** In every relationship — with customers, with partners, with each other.

Trust is not a feature. It is not a value on a slide deck. It is the contract we enter into every time a business hands us access to their data, their workflows, their people.

**Personal responsibility.** We own what we do. If something breaks, we fix it. If we said we'd do something, we do it. No hiding behind process or policy.

**Serious, not solemn.** Serious business does not mean joyless. Quite the opposite. We do serious work, we have fun doing it, and we celebrate when it works.

---

## What We Are Not

We are not a VC-funded tool that pivots in 18 months.
We are not a demo that gets forgotten after the kick-off call.
We are not a vendor. We are a partner.

F.X. Meiller is not buying a product.
They are placing trust in us.
That is a different contract entirely.

---

## The Promise

A business that works with Wren gets more than software.
They get a team that understands their world, respects their scale, and shows up.

AI that earns its place in how a company actually works.
Not because it's clever. Because it's reliable.
